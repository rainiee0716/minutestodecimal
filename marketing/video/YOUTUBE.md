Payroll wants decimal hours, but the clock gives you hours and minutes. This is the free
converter we built to fix that mismatch — used here by real managers every week.

What you get (all free, no account needed):
- Minutes to decimal converter: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/
- Weekly timesheet calculator (lunch breaks + overtime split): https://www.minutestodecimal.org/weekly-timesheet-calculator
- Full 1–60 conversion chart: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/minutes-to-decimal-chart
- Printable one-page PDF chart: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/minutes-to-decimal-chart.pdf

The formula behind everything in the video:
decimal hours = hours + (minutes ÷ 60)
7h 45m = 7 + (45 ÷ 60) = 7.75 — not 7.45.
