## Project name

Minutes to Decimal

## Short description

Free browser-based time card and payroll calculators.

## Long description

Minutes to Decimal is a free collection of online tools for converting work time into the decimal hours used by payroll systems.

Features include:

- Minutes to decimal hours conversion
- Hours and minutes to decimal hours
- Decimal hours back to hours and minutes
- Weekly timesheet calculation
- Unpaid lunch deduction
- Regular and overtime hour splitting
- Time card rounding calculators
- Full 1–60 minute conversion chart

The tools run entirely in the browser. There is no sign-up, no installation, and no calculator data uploaded to a server.

Website: https://www.minutestodecimal.org
