# BetaList submission copy

## Startup name

Minutes to Decimal

## Tagline

Free time card and payroll calculators for hourly workers.

## Description

Minutes to Decimal is a free collection of browser-based tools that turn clock times and work durations into payroll-ready decimal hours.

It helps hourly employees, freelancers, and small business owners:

- Convert minutes to decimal hours
- Convert decimal hours back to hours and minutes
- Calculate a full weekly timesheet
- Subtract unpaid lunch breaks
- Split regular and overtime hours
- Estimate gross pay
- Compare common time card rounding rules

Everything runs in the browser. There is no sign-up, no download, and no calculator input sent to a server.

Website: https://www.minutestodecimal.org

## Why now

Payroll systems expect decimal hours, but most people still think in hours and minutes. That mismatch causes avoidable mistakes on timesheets, invoices, and pay stubs.

## Ideal user

- Hourly employees
- Freelancers billing by the hour
- Small business owners
- HR and operations staff
