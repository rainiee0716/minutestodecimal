# 目录站 / AlternativeTo 提交文案（dofollow 杠杆）

> 用途：这批评测站的外链大多 **dofollow（或至少会被 Google 索引）**，比 Reddit/Quora 的 nofollow 链接更能传权重，是 7 个未收录页排名的真正杠杆。
> 锚文本纪律：用品牌名「Minutes to Decimal」或裸链 `https://www.minutestodecimal.org`，**不要用** "minutes to decimal converter" 这类精准商业词做锚（避免过优化）。
> 节奏：分散在 2–4 周发，不要同天狂发。

- **Short description（≤160 字符，用于 meta/简介）**：
  Free browser-based time card and payroll calculators. Convert work hours to decimal payroll format, with charts, rounding, and timesheet tools. No signup.
- **Medium description（表单主描述，1–2 句）**：
  Minutes to Decimal is a free suite of browser-based time conversion and payroll calculators. It helps hourly employees, freelancers, and small business owners convert work hours into the decimal format payroll systems expect — plus a 1–60 minute chart, time-card rounding, and weekly timesheet tools. No signup required.
- **Long / About（长描述，填「关于」类字段时用）**：
  Minutes to Decimal is a free collection of browser-based time conversion and payroll calculators. It covers minutes↔decimal hours, hours↔decimal, a full 1–60 minute conversion chart, weekly timesheet calculation, unpaid lunch deduction, regular/overtime splitting, and time-card rounding comparison. Everything runs in the browser with no account and no tracking — built for hourly staff, freelancers, and small teams who need payroll-ready numbers fast.
