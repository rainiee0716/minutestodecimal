# External channel launch kit

Purpose: ready-to-use copy and content outlines for promoting minutestodecimal.org on platforms other than Product Hunt and AlternativeTo.

## Priority order

1. LinkedIn company page
2. DEV.to article
3. Hashnode article
4. Medium article
5. SlideShare deck
6. Issuu document
7. About.me profile
8. SourceForge project listing
9. BetaList submission

Product Hunt and AlternativeTo are already submitted, so they are intentionally excluded from this kit.

## Recommended link targets

- Brand/home: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/
- Tools hub: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/tools
- Weekly calculator: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/weekly-timesheet-calculator
- Chart: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/minutes-to-decimal-chart
- Overtime guide: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/guides/overtime-decimal-hours
- Biweekly guide: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/guides/biweekly-timesheet-guide
- Rounding guide: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/guides/time-card-rounding

## Rules

- Do not copy the website guides word-for-word.
- Use each platform's native tone.
- Link to the most relevant tool page, not always the homepage.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Keep the tone practical and human.

## Not prioritized

The following platforms are not prioritized for the current site positioning:

- Crunchbase: better suited to companies with formal organization and funding data.
- Wellfound / AngelList: better suited to hiring or venture-backed startups.
- G2 / Capterra: better suited to SaaS products with user reviews and sales workflows.
- Clutch: better suited to service agencies, not free utility tools.
- Muck Rack: better suited to journalists and PR professionals.
- Disqus: low value unless actively participating in relevant communities.

These can be revisited later if the product positioning changes.
