# Behance case study copy

## Project title

Minutes to Decimal — Free Time Card & Payroll Tools

## Subtitle

A utility-first web product for converting work hours into payroll-ready decimal hours.

## Project summary

Minutes to Decimal is a free collection of browser-based calculators for timesheets, payroll, and time card math. The product focuses on one clear job: helping people turn real work hours into the decimal format payroll systems expect.

The project includes:

- A clean tools directory
- Minutes-to-decimal conversion
- Weekly timesheet calculations
- Regular and overtime hour splitting
- Time card rounding comparisons
- Full conversion charts
- Plain-language payroll guides

## Case study outline

1. Problem
   - Clock times and payroll hours are different formats
   - People confuse 4.35 hours with 4 hours 35 minutes

2. Audience
   - Hourly employees
   - Freelancers
   - Small business owners
   - HR and operations teams

3. Product goals
   - Make the answer visible immediately
   - Explain the formula, not just the result
   - Support real timesheet workflows
   - Keep everything free and browser-based

4. Design approach
   - Utility-first layout
   - Clear hierarchy
   - Minimal visual noise
   - Mobile-friendly forms
   - Accessible labels and live results

5. Key screens
   - Homepage
   - Tools directory
   - Weekly Timesheet Calculator
   - Minutes to decimal chart
   - Time card rounding calculator

6. Results and next steps
   - Growing tool set
   - Expanding payroll templates and guides
