## Description

Minutes to Decimal is a free collection of browser-based time conversion and payroll tools. It helps hourly employees, freelancers, and small business owners convert work hours into the decimal format payroll systems expect.

The site includes calculators for:

- Minutes to decimal hours
- Time to decimal hours
- Weekly timesheets
- Regular and overtime hours
- Time card rounding
- Decimal-to-hours conversion
- Full minutes-to-decimal charts

Everything runs in the browser. There is no sign-up, no installation, and no user data collection from calculator inputs.

Website: https://www.minutestodecimal.org
