# About.me profile copy

## Headline

Free time conversion and payroll calculators

## Short bio

I build simple, privacy-friendly tools that help hourly employees, freelancers, and small businesses convert work time into payroll-ready decimal hours.

## Longer bio

Minutes to Decimal is a free collection of browser-based calculators for time cards, weekly timesheets, overtime, and payroll math. The tools convert hours and minutes into decimal hours, split regular and overtime pay, and explain the formulas behind the results.

Everything runs locally in your browser. There are no accounts, no downloads, and no calculator data sent to a server.

Explore the tools: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/tools
