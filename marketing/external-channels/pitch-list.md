# 外链 Pitch 清单（2026-11-09 整理）

目标：给 chart 页 / PDF chart 拿到第一批真实外链。PDF 是钩子，语气是"送资源"不是"求链接"。

## 邮件模板（一封打天下，改 [Name] 和 [site] 即可）

> Subject: Free printable minutes-to-decimal chart for your readers
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I maintain a free timesheet conversion tool set at minutestodecimal.org. We just published a one-page printable minutes-to-decimal chart (PDF) — the full 1-60 table plus quarter-hour rounding rules, no signup, and nothing on it but a small footer link back to us.
>
> If it fits your [resources page / roundup / guide], feel free to link or host it for your audience: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/minutes-to-decimal-chart.pdf
>
> Either way, keep up the good work on [site name].
>
> [你的名字]

要点：一段半以内；只推 PDF 这一个资产；不提 SEO、不提交换。
