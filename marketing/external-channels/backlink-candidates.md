## Operating rules

- Do not submit any site without a separate per-site approval.
- Do not use paid placement, reciprocal links, or exact-match commercial anchors.
- Use `Minutes to Decimal` or the naked URL as the public anchor.
- Record every attempt in `backlink-campaign-v2.md`.
- Treat a successful submission as `submitted`, not `published`, until the public listing is verified.
