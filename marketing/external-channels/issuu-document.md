### Page 2: Decimal hours explained

- Formula: decimal hours = hours + (minutes ÷ 60)
- Examples:
  - 15 minutes = 0.25
  - 20 minutes = 0.33
  - 30 minutes = 0.50
  - 45 minutes = 0.75
- Warning: 4.35 is not 4 hours 35 minutes

### Page 3: Weekly timesheet workflow

1. Record clock-in and clock-out times
2. Subtract unpaid lunch
3. Convert each day to decimal hours
4. Add the weekly total
5. Compare with the overtime threshold
6. Check the pay stub
