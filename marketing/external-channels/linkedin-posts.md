## Post 2: Common mistake

4.35 hours is not 4 hours 35 minutes.

It is 4 hours and 21 minutes.

Why?
0.35 × 60 = 21 minutes.

If you worked 4 hours 35 minutes, the decimal is:
4 + (35 ÷ 60) = 4.58

## Post 3: Minutes-to-decimal chart

The four payroll landmarks worth memorizing:

- 15 minutes = 0.25
- 30 minutes = 0.50
- 45 minutes = 0.75
- 60 minutes = 1.00

## Post 4: Overtime

A weekly total of 43.75 hours is not just “43.75 regular hours.”

Under a 40-hour threshold:

- Regular hours: 40.00
- Overtime hours: 3.75

Guide: https://www.minutestodecimal.org/guides/overtime-decimal-hours
