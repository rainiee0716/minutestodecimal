# SlideShare deck kit

## Deck title

Minutes to Decimal Hours: A Payroll Cheat Sheet

## Subtitle

Convert timesheet minutes into payroll-ready decimal hours in one page.

## Recommended length

10–12 slides.

## Slide-by-slide outline

1. Title slide
   - Minutes to Decimal Hours: A Payroll Cheat Sheet
   - Free tools at minutestodecimal.org

2. Why payroll uses decimal hours
   - Payroll multiplies hours by a rate
   - Decimal hours make the math simple

3. The formula
   - decimal hours = hours + (minutes ÷ 60)

4. The four landmarks
   - 15 minutes = 0.25
   - 30 minutes = 0.50
   - 45 minutes = 0.75
   - 60 minutes = 1.00

5. Common mistake
   - 4.35 is not 4 hours 35 minutes
   - It is 4 hours and 21 minutes

6. Worked example
   - 7 hours 45 minutes
   - 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75
   - Total: 7.75 hours

7. Weekly timesheet example
   - 8.00 + 7.50 + 8.25 + 7.75 + 8.00
   - Total: 39.50 hours

8. Overtime example
   - Weekly total: 43.75
   - Regular: 40.00
   - Overtime: 3.75

9. Rounding reminder
   - Some employers round to 5, 6, 10, or 15 minutes
   - Check the rule before comparing totals

10. Quick chart
    - Show a compact 1–60 minute conversion table

11. Free tools
    - Minutes to Decimal Converter
    - Weekly Timesheet Calculator
    - Time Card Rounding Calculator

12. Call to action
    - Try the free tools:
      https://www.minutestodecimal.org/tools

## Description

A quick cheat sheet for converting minutes to decimal hours on timesheets and payroll records. Includes common conversions, worked examples, overtime math, and free online calculators.
