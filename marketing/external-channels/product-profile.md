## Approved public description

Minutes to Decimal is a free collection of browser-based time conversion and payroll calculators. It helps hourly employees, freelancers, and small business owners convert work hours into the decimal format payroll systems expect.

## Core features

- Minutes to decimal hours conversion
- Hours and minutes to decimal hours
- Decimal hours back to hours and minutes
- Weekly timesheet calculation
- Unpaid lunch deduction
- Regular and overtime hour splitting
- Time card rounding comparison
- Full 1–60 minute conversion chart
